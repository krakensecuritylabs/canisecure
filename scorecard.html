Security Scorecard

A quick self-assessment pulled from across all CaniSecure guides. Check off what you have done, identify what is left, and follow the links to the full guides for step-by-step instructions.

No one gets 100% on day one. Pick a section, work through it, and come back for the next one.

Passwords & Authentication

Full guide: Passwords

  • ☐ Using a password manager (Bitwarden or 1Password)
  • ☐ Master passphrase is 21+ characters of random words
  • ☐ 2FA enabled on password manager (hardware key or authenticator app)
  • ☐ Every account has a unique, generated password (no reuse)
  • ☐ Passkeys enabled on services that support them
  • ☐ Breach monitoring set up at Have I Been Pwned
  • ☐ Old or weak passwords identified and rotated

Social Engineering Awareness

Full guide: Social Engineering Protection

  • ☐ I know to slow down when a message or call creates urgency
  • ☐ I verify unexpected requests through a separate, trusted channel
  • ☐ I have a family code word for emergency calls
  • ☐ I do not click links in unexpected texts or emails — I go to the site directly
  • ☐ I never share MFA codes with anyone who contacts me

AI Tools

Full guide: AI Tools & Chatbots

  • ☐ I do not share passwords, financial data, or government IDs with AI tools
  • ☐ Opted out of data training on every AI platform I use
  • ☐ AI accounts secured with strong passphrase and 2FA
  • ☐ Conversation history cleared regularly
  • ☐ I verify AI-generated facts before acting on them

Devices

Full guides: Personal Computer · Mobile Phone

  • ☐ Operating system and apps are set to auto-update
  • ☐ Full-disk encryption enabled (FileVault, BitLocker, or LUKS)
  • ☐ Screen lock enabled with a strong passphrase or biometrics
  • ☐ Find My Device / remote wipe enabled
  • ☐ Unused apps removed

Accounts & Privacy

Full guides: Digital Life · Public Records

  • ☐ Privacy settings reviewed on major platforms (Google, Apple, Microsoft, social media)
  • ☐ Location sharing disabled or limited to trusted contacts only
  • ☐ Data broker opt-outs submitted (or using an automated removal service)
  • ☐ Connected apps and third-party access audited and pruned
  • ☐ Recovery email and phone number up to date on critical accounts

Home & Network

Full guides: Home Office · IoT Devices

  • ☐ Router firmware updated and default credentials changed
  • ☐ WiFi using WPA3 (or WPA2 minimum) with a strong passphrase
  • ☐ IoT devices on a separate network or VLAN
  • ☐ Smart home device microphones and cameras reviewed
  • ☐ Guest network set up for visitors

Travel

Full guide: Travel Security

  • ☐ I know my rights regarding device searches at borders
  • ☐ I use a VPN on untrusted WiFi networks
  • ☐ Biometric unlock disabled before crossing borders
  • ☐ Travel eSIM or local SIM plan in place for international trips

Backups & Recovery

  • ☐ Automated backups running for important files and photos
  • ☐ Backups stored in at least two locations (one off-site or cloud)
  • ☐ Recovery codes for critical accounts saved securely
  • ☐ Tested restoring from backup at least once

How to use this scorecard

Print this page or bookmark it. Work through one section at a time — each checkbox links to the full guide with step-by-step instructions. Come back periodically (every 3–6 months) and run through it again. Security is not a one-time setup.
